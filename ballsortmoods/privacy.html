Privacy Policy – Ball Sort Moods

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Ball Sort Moods ("the game") is made by YouJavu ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

What we collect

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location. You do not need an account to play.

Your game progress (levels completed, Mood Coins, items you own and your settings) is stored only on your device. We do not receive it.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • basic device information (model, operating system, language),
  • your approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • how you interact with ads,
  • crash and performance information.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Players in the UK and EU are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Sticker packs

The game includes sticker packs for Messages (iPhone) and WhatsApp (Android). Two packs come inside the game; the others are unlocked with Mood Coins.

  • When you open or unlock a pack, the game downloads its sticker pictures from our website, youjavu.com (hosted by GitHub Pages). Like any website, the server receives your device's IP address and basic request details to deliver the files. The game adds no ID or personal information to these downloads.
  • The stickers are saved only on your device. On iPhone, the game's Messages sticker extension reads them from the game's own storage. On Android, WhatsApp reads a pack only when you tap Add to WhatsApp.
  • We never see which stickers you send, or to whom.

Sharing

The Share button opens your phone's own share menu. We don't see what you share or who you share it with. The small once-a-day coin gift for pressing Share is counted on your device only.

Purchases

Purchases (Remove forced ads, coin packs, Starter bundle, Legend Pack) are handled entirely by Google Play on Android and the App Store on iPhone. We never see or store your payment details. The store tells the game only which item was bought, so it can be delivered and restored.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. The only third parties involved are Google (ads and Google Play purchases), Apple (App Store purchases) and GitHub (which hosts our website and sticker files), as described above.

Your choices

  • Uninstalling the game deletes all game data stored on your device.
  • You can turn off personalised ads in your device settings.

Changes

We may update this policy. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
