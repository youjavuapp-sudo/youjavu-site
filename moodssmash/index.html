The sorting machine forgot closing time and locked the Moods' friends in glass jars. Aim the launcher, fire a Mood at a jar of its own colour and smash their friends free.

Smash, chain, rescue

  • Fire each Mood at a jar of its own colour: touching jars of the same colour burst together in big chains
  • 600 levels in 10 worlds, with big boss levels along the way
  • Jars on swings, balloons, coasters and slides, and jars that vanish and come back
  • Treasure jars, mystery jars and a jackpot to find
  • The 14 Moods from Ball Sort Moods, each with their own colour
  • Boosters when you're stuck, a gift every day, and hats, launchers and effects to dress up your Moods
  • Two special packs of 100 extra-hard levels: Dozy's Dreamland and Café Delivery Disaster
  • Free Moods stickers to send in Messages and WhatsApp

Free to play, with no ads. Coins and the two level packs are optional purchases.
