Privacy Policy – Moods Smash

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Moods Smash ("the game") is made by YouJavu ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

What we collect

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location. You do not need an account to play.

Your game progress (levels completed, stars, coins, boosters, items you own, sticker packs and your settings) is stored only on your device. We do not receive it.

Advertising and tracking

The game shows no ads and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software. It does not use your device's advertising ID. If we ever add ads in a future update, we will update this policy before that update is released.

Sticker packs

The game includes sticker packs for Messages (iPhone) and WhatsApp (Android). Two packs come inside the game; the others are unlocked with coins.

  • When you open or unlock a pack, the game downloads its sticker pictures from our website, youjavu.com (hosted by GitHub Pages). Like any website, the server receives your device's IP address and basic request details to deliver the files. The game adds no ID or personal information to these downloads.
  • The stickers are saved only on your device. On iPhone, the game's Messages sticker extension reads them from the game's own storage. On Android, WhatsApp reads a pack only when you tap Add to WhatsApp.
  • We never see which stickers you send, or to whom.

Sharing, photos and feedback

  • The Share button opens your phone's own share menu. We don't see what you share or who you share it with. On Android, the small once-a-day coin gift for sharing is counted on your device only.
  • On iPhone, if you choose Save Image in the share menu, your phone asks your permission to add that picture to your Photos. The game never reads your photos.
  • "Send feedback" opens your own email app. We only receive what you choose to send us.
  • Now and then the game may ask whether you'd like to rate it. The rating window is shown by Google Play or the App Store, not by us.

Purchases

Purchases (coin packs and the Dozy's Dreamland and Café Delivery Disaster level packs) are handled entirely by Google Play on Android and the App Store on iPhone. We never see or store your payment details. The store tells the game only which item was bought, so it can be delivered and restored.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. The only third parties involved are Google (Google Play purchases), Apple (App Store purchases) and GitHub (which hosts our website and sticker files), as described above.

Your choices

  • Uninstalling the game deletes all game data stored on your device.

Changes

We may update this policy. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
