Privacy Policy – Sand Gates

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Sand Gates ("the game") is made by YouJavu ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

What we collect

We do not collect any personal information. We don't collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location, and you do not need an account to play.

Your game progress (levels completed, stars, the pictures in your album and your settings) is stored only on your device. We do not receive it.

Advertising and tracking

The game shows no ads and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software. It does not use your device's advertising ID. If we ever add ads in a future update, we will update this policy before that update is released.

Internet

The game does not need the internet to play. The "Privacy policy" link in Settings opens this page in your web browser.

Purchases

The game has no purchases.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data sharing

We do not collect, sell or share personal information.

Your choices

Changes

We may update this policy. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
