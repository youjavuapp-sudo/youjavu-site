Privacy Policy – Tile Café Trip

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Tile Café Trip ("the game") is made by YouJavu ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

What we collect

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location. You do not need an account to play.

Your game progress (levels completed, coins, boosters, decorations, packs and your settings) is stored only on your device. We do not receive it.

Advertising

The game may show ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • basic device information (model, operating system, language),
  • your approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • how you interact with ads,
  • crash and performance information.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Players in the UK and EU are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. On iPhone, the advertising identifier is used only if you allow it when Apple asks.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

Purchases (coin bags, the Welcome Basket, No ads and paid level packs) are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your payment details. The store tells the game only which item was bought, so it can be delivered and restored.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. The only third parties involved are Google (ads, and purchases on Android) and Apple (purchases on iPhone), as described above.

Your choices

  • Uninstalling the game deletes all game data stored on your device.
  • You can turn off personalised ads in your device settings.

Changes

We may update this policy. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
