Privacy Policy – Tile Café Trip

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Tile Café Trip ("the game") is made by YouJavu ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

What we collect

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location. You do not need an account to play.

Your game progress (levels completed, coins, boosters, decorations, packs and your settings) is stored only on your device. We do not receive it.

Advertising and tracking

The game shows no ads and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software. It does not use your device's advertising ID. If we ever add ads in a future update, we will update this policy before that update is released.

Purchases

Purchases (coin bags, the Welcome Basket and paid level packs) are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your payment details. The store tells the game only which item was bought, so it can be delivered and restored.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. The only third parties involved are Google (purchases on Android) and Apple (purchases on iPhone), as described above.

Your choices

  • Uninstalling the game deletes all game data stored on your device.

Changes

We may update this policy. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
